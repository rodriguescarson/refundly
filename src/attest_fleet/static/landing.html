How it works

Three moves. The third is the one nobody else makes.

Most agents stop at the second step and report success. Refundly's job starts at the third.

01

Say it in plain words

“I was charged twice for my gym and I want it stopped and refunded.” No forms, no case numbers — the agent works out who you are and what has to happen.

02

It acts through PayPal

The refund and the cancellation, carried out through PayPal's own REST APIs in sandbox. Risky amounts pause for your say-so first.

03

It verifies against the ledger

Before saying “done,” Refundly reads PayPal's record directly — not the tool's reply — and confirms the state actually changed. If it didn't, you hear about it.
